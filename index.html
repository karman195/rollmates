Rollmates

Ludo and Snakes & Ladders with friends anywhere in the world. Free, no ads, no sign-up.

Play with friends

Create Room picks the game and how many players (Ludo 2–6, Snakes & Ladders 2–4) and gives you a 6-digit room code. Share it; friends tap Join Room and type the code. The host taps Start Game. Empty seats can be filled with computer players.

Other ways to play

vs Computer — play against the computer (Casual or Smart).
Pass & Play — everyone shares one phone or tablet.

Rules in short

Ludo: roll a 6 to leave your yard; land on an opponent to send them home; stars and start squares are safe; a 6, a capture or reaching home earns another roll; three 6s in a row lose the turn; you need the exact roll to finish.
Snakes & Ladders: climb ladders, slide down snakes, a 6 rolls again, exact roll to land on 100.

Turn timer and connection

Online turns have a 15-second timer. If you miss three turns, autoplay takes over until you tap to play again. If your connection drops, open the app again to rejoin the same game.

Questions or problems

Open an issue at github.com/karman195/rollmates/issues and we'll get back to you.

Rollmates is free, with no ads, no purchases and no account. Ludo and Snakes & Ladders are traditional games; Rollmates is not affiliated with any other game or company.
