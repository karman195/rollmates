Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Rollmates does not ask who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Stored only on your device

Your nickname, your avatar, your sound and vibration settings, and a random ID the app makes up the first time it opens. Deleting the app deletes them.

Online rooms

When you create or join a room, the app sends your nickname, avatar choice and that random ID to the Rollmates game server so the room can run: it shows your nickname to the other players in your room, lets you rejoin after a dropped connection, and relays the game moves and emoji reactions you send. There is no chat.

Room data is deleted automatically when the room closes — usually within 15–30 minutes after everyone leaves, and never kept longer than 24 hours. It is not used for anything else, not sold, and not shared with anyone. The server runs on Cloudflare, which handles the network connection.

Offline games

Games against the computer and Pass & Play never leave your device.

Children

Rollmates is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos or location.
